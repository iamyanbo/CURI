# Finance research-driven builder

Develop and implement potentially novel mechanisms for robust systematic
decision-making, emphasizing regime dependence, transaction costs, capacity,
selection effects, and failure modes rather than maximizing one backtest
number. Begin by mapping the nearest admitted prior art and establishing honest
baselines. For every proposed mechanism, state its exact intended difference,
the competing explanation, a falsifier, and the evidence needed to distinguish
them.

Use an artifact program when an idea requires a reusable multi-task
implementation. Build it through independently verified checkpoints, then
study its mechanism. Aggregations of known signals are acceptable only when the
interaction, selector, risk allocation, or regime mechanism is itself the
hypothesis. Do not call an implementation novel merely because it differs from
the seed or improves a score; novelty is a scoped comparison to the nearest
prior work and may be refuted.

The current finance domain is a deterministic synthetic mechanism benchmark,
not real-market alpha evidence. Its five anonymous folds are protected
confirmation evidence, not an iterative tuning oracle. Record every related
attempt, including failed variants, and distinguish exploration from
confirmation. Retain negative and bounded findings. A later real-data phase
must use point-in-time data, realistic frictions and baselines, a frozen final
holdout, multiple-testing correction, and prospective paper trading before any
claim about deployable edge.

## Fixed constraints

- Candidate decisions are causal and use only information available at that
  decision time.
- Harness-owned folds, costs, holding shift, and protected measurements never
  move in response to results.
- Every result reports turnover, costs, tested regimes, trial count, and what
  does not follow from the evidence.
- Protected confirmation does not automatically promote an incumbent or choose
  the next task.
- A reusable checkpoint requires independently rerun passing checks and a
  coherent capability justified independently of metric improvement.

## Open research directions

- Regime representation and regime-conditional behavior.
- Transaction-cost, turnover, and capacity sensitivity.
- Selection bias and multiple-testing effects in autonomous strategy search.
- Position sizing, risk scaling, cross-asset structure, and signal interaction.
- Mechanisms that predict when a simple strategy or aggregation should fail.
